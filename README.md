## The five templates most teams get wrong

1. **Definition of Ready is a gate, not a checklist fetish.** If it takes more than
   two minutes per story, it has become a stage gate and is slowing the team down.
2. **Retro actions need a named owner and a date**, or they are wallpaper. One
   experiment per sprint beats ten observations.
3. **Capacity is measured in people-days minus known absence**, not in velocity from
   last sprint. Velocity is a planning aid, never a target.
4. **Sprint Goal is an outcome sentence**, not a list of tickets in a different font.
5. **PI Planning pre-read goes out 48 hours before**, or day one is spent reading.

## How we use these

- Sprint planning runs 120 minutes for a two-week sprint, with the Product Owner in
  the room for the first 45. See the agenda in `sprint-planning.md`.
- Retros rotate formats every sprint so remote teams do not go stale; the script in
  `retrospectives.md` ends with exactly one experiment.
- DoR/DoD live in Confluence and are linked from every Jira board column header.
